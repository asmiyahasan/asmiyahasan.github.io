Other projects

  • Multimodal plan recognition for adaptive assistance in AR

    My master's thesis. A probabilistic model that combines eye gaze and hand movements to work out which recipe someone is following, and which step they're on, in real time (95.8% accuracy). It also detects mistakes and hesitation, flagging 78% of the moments when users needed help. Data came from a 3D kitchen I built in Unity.

    Python, Unity, VR eye and hand tracking, machine learning

  • Robotic pick-and-place with deep reinforcement learning

    Trained a SAC policy in MuJoCo for a UR5e robot arm, reaching 60% pick-and-place success over 2.4M steps, and built a four-node ROS 2 stack to run the trained policy on real hardware without retraining.

    Python, PyTorch, MuJoCo, ROS 2

  • Reflexo

    A native iOS app with four reflex-training mini-games, sign-in, global leaderboards, offline score history and a home-screen widget. Built with a teammate.

    Swift, SwiftUI, SwiftData, WidgetKit, Firebase
