Predator vs Prey

Two agents that taught themselves to hunt and to hide, trained by self-play reinforcement learning. They run right here in your browser. Pick a side and play against one.

How it was built

Loading the agents…

Move with the arrow keys or WASD; hold two keys to move diagonally. Press Space to start. Until you do, the two agents play each other. Playing needs a keyboard, so on a phone you can watch the demo.

Teach an agent tic-tac-toe

This agent starts out knowing only the rules. Every game you play against it, it updates its opinion of each position it has seen, so if you beat it the same way twice it starts to block you. Or let it practise against itself and watch the chart climb.
